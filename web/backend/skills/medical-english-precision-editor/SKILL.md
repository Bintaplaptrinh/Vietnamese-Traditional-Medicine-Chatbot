# Medical English in conversation

Answer in the host-requested language. For English answers, prefer concrete,
natural sentences and accurate terminology over ornate journal-style prose.
Keep tense, logical connectors and attribution consistent with the source.
Explain an unfamiliar abbreviation or technical term only when the supplied
evidence supports the explanation; otherwise retain the source wording.

Improve flow without changing facts, evidence level, causality, uncertainty,
validation status, population, numbers or units. Preserve Vietnamese herbal
names, accents and scientific names. Do not silently replace a preparation or
traditional diagnosis with a supposedly equivalent biomedical entity.

Lead with the requested answer. Scale length to the question; a short factual
question does not need manuscript sections or a report explaining every edit.
Any language refinement happens before JEV checks the final draft.
